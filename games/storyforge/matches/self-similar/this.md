# self-similar

A solo, adult (m35) liminal-horror / existential-crisis match. One man, an empty corporate tower, a late shift that stops ending. **Status: 🟡 In progress — Act I, turn 0/20.**

## Summary

The player is alone on the 41st floor of a corporate tower after hours. The elevator opens onto a floor that isn't quite the one he left. It's not madness and it's not a haunting -- the building is a real iterated function system, folding a small floor plan into smaller, warped copies of itself forever, and something about the recursion is keyed to him. The disruptive concept is **fractal geometry & iterated function systems** (self-similarity, fractal dimension, attractors, fixed points), taught as the literal mechanism of the space and, by Act IV, revealed as the mechanism of a life that keeps generating the same shape from the same rule.

## Config notes (deviations from the player's literal ask)

- **Rating capped at `PG-13`.** The player asked for "no rating limits"; the schema's ceiling is `PG-13` (no gore, no sexual explicitness). Intensity is carried by dread, recursion, and existential weight, not graphic content.
- **`depth: existential`** is the schema's top value. The player's "4D fractal" depth is implemented as a craft technique on top of it: the story's structure itself is self-similar across scales (turn-level beats echo act-level beats; the building's recursion depth doubles as elapsed time/iteration count -- see `lore.md`), rather than as a fourth settings value.
