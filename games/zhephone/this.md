# zhephone

A permadeath MMO built inside the AIOS framework. Concept and economic design in progress — no code yet.

## Identity

Zhephone is a "permadeath Tibia" crossed with a real-money royale: a persistent PvE world where players band into clans and alliances, and rivalry between those clans is the only channel through which PvP happens. The game runs as a once-a-year live event (a concentrated multi-day season, not a year-long grind — see population math in `.this/memory.md`), ending with a small pool of survivors who split a sponsor-funded cash prize.

## North star

- Death must be permanent and must matter economically — the tension is the whole point.
- PvP is never free-for-all; it exists only through clan/alliance rivalry layered on top of a PvE base game.
- Real money drives genuine social behavior (trust, betrayal, alliances) — but the prize structure must stay inside legal prize-contest territory, not gambling. See "Prize structure" in `.this/memory.md`.
- Combat/death outcomes should be skill-predominant, not RNG-predominant — this is both a design and a legal requirement.

## Legal constraints (load-bearing, not optional)

- **No player-paid entry fee** — kills the "consideration" prong of gambling law. Free to play, free to compete.
- **Sponsor-funded prize pool**, disclosed openly (tournament-purse model, like esports) — not disguised as an "art" purchase or laundered through opaque intermediaries. That idea was considered and explicitly rejected; see memory log.
- **Skill-predominant permadeath** — minimize chance in combat resolution to stay on the right side of skill-vs-chance tests in stricter jurisdictions.
- Not legal advice; a real gaming-law attorney is required before any real-money implementation ships.
